# Terrapin Works Electric Boat Team — Research Overview

## Team Identity
- **Full name:** Terrapin Works Electric Boat Team (TWEBT)
- **Parent org:** Terrapin Works, UMD's A. James Clark School of Engineering makerspace (founded 2014, 200+ machines, 28,000 sq ft)
- **Founded:** Spring 2026
- **Size:** ~25 active members spanning Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, and Computer Science
- **Workspace:** Alex Garage + Terrapin Works fabrication labs

## Competition Focus
- **Primary:** ASNE Promoting Electric Propulsion (PEP) East 2027 regatta, Portsmouth, VA — manned displacement class
- **Target:** Electric hydrofoil conversion of a 10'6" UFO carbon-composite catamaran, 20+ knots sustained flight
- **PEP grant confirmed:** $7,000 (ASNE new-team grant)

## Technical Scope
- **Propulsion:** Dual Flipsky 65220 integrated motor/ESC units, custom retractable motor leg through the mast socket
- **Battery:** 15S×8P lithium-ion pack (Reliant or TENPOWER cells) with JKBMS, Pelican-case enclosure
- **Foil system:** Stock UFO wand-controlled mechanical front foil + manual rear trim, with future autonomy path
- **Control:** GitHub-branched CAD workflow (SOLIDWORKS), 3D scanning + photogrammetry for reverse engineering, MATLAB motor-torque modeling
- **CAD leads:** Emerson (foil/strut), Justin (motor placement), Carter (scans)
