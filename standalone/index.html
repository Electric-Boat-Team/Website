Foil system

A system with
its own place.

The foil system is retained independently within the boat design. Working through its interfaces means considering the surrounding structure, the available space, and how the pieces fit together.

Design input
An independently retained foil system, considered alongside the electric propulsion layout.

Rudder & clearance

Leave room
for the details.

A component cannot be considered in isolation. The rudder is part of the same physical boat as the propulsion and foil systems, so clearance and routing remain part of the design conversation.
