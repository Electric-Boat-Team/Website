An electric conversion.

Our starting point is a UFO carbon-composite catamaran. The conversion replaces sail propulsion with a motor leg while retaining the boat's stock foil system.

A motor leg through
the mast socket.

The planned propulsion interface uses the existing mast socket. Motor cooling, cable routing, and clearance from the adjacent front strut opening are under study.

Keep the foils
independent.

The stock foil system is retained independently of the motor leg. The front strut section helps us study its geometry and the clearance needed alongside the propulsion installation.
