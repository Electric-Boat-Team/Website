Sponsors

Supporting the team backs more than a boat. It develops the next generation of marine engineers and builds a pipeline to students already turning ambitious ideas into working hardware.

Where the money goes

All sponsorship capital is directed to flight-critical hardware, raw materials, safety gear, and precision tooling. Contributions are routed through official University of Maryland departmental accounts, so gifts are tax-exempt under the university's 501(c)(3) status. There is no club overhead.
