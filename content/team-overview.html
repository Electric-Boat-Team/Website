Build process

From CAD to competition

The team works in GitHub-branched CAD, SOLIDWORKS parametric foil models, and shared design documents. Scans of the driver area, foil mounts, and mast socket feed the motor-leg and retraction geometry. PEP registration as a new team secures the $7,000 grant and competition slot.
