Sub-teams

Propulsion

Dual Flipsky 65220 motors, custom retractable motor leg through the mast socket, strut integration, propeller clearance and cooling.

Power & Energy

15S×8P lithium battery pack, JKBMS, nickel strip and copper bus bars, dielectric coolant in Pelican-case enclosure.

Hull & Structures

Carbon-composite UFO catamaran conversion, foil system, mast socket, 3D scanning, photogrammetry, and SOLIDWORKS CAD.

Controls & Autonomy

Motor controllers, GPS receivers, electronic steering, autonomous way-point following as a drop-in upgrade.

Testing & Safety

On-water testing, trim tuning, data analysis, safety systems, and competition logistics.

Build process

From CAD to competition

GitHub-branched CAD, SOLIDWORKS models, and photogrammetry scans feed the motor-leg and retraction geometry. PEP registration secures the $7,000 grant.
