The project

A boat with a purpose

Our current project is an electric hydrofoil based on a lightweight carbon-composite catamaran, targeting sustained flight at 20+ knots for the ASNE Promoting Electric Propulsion (PEP) East 2027 regatta. The work brings mechanical design, electrical systems, fabrication, and testing together around one vessel.

Electric propulsion means quieter operation and no gasoline or engine oil on board. For students, it is a chance to learn by building something real.
