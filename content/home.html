The project

UFO hydrofoil conversion

The team is converting a carbon-composite UFO catamaran into an electric hydrofoil. The project spans a custom motor leg through the mast socket, a 15S×8P lithium-ion battery pack, Flipsky 65220 motors, and a wand-controlled foil system.

Five sub-teams, Controls & Autonomy, Hull & Structures, Power & Energy, Propulsion, and Testing, work from the Alex Garage workspace.
