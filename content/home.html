Clean propulsive power belongs on the water

The Terrapin Works Electric Boat Team designs, builds, tests, and races electric marine propulsion systems at the University of Maryland.

Marine combustion engines have never faced the emissions bar that cars have. Boats push through water roughly 800 times denser than air, run under heavy sustained loads, and release exhaust straight into the places people swim, paddle, and fish. Electric motors are simpler, quieter, and cleaner — no gasoline, no engine oil, no fuel spills at the dock.

From the Chesapeake Bay, we are converting an ultra-light carbon-composite catamaran into an electric hydrofoil for the ASNE Promoting Electric Propulsion (PEP) East 2027 regatta.
