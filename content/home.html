The project

UFO hydrofoil conversion

The team is converting a carbon-composite UFO sailing catamaran into an electric hydrofoil. The project spans a custom retractable motor leg through the mast socket, a 15S×8P lithium-ion battery pack, Flipsky 65220 dual motors, and a mechanical wand-controlled foil system.

Five integrated sub-teams — Controls & Autonomy, Hull & Structures, Power & Energy, Propulsion & Powertrain, and Testing & Safety — work together from the Alex Garage workspace.
