Made at Terrapin Works.

A Maryland project

Where ideas become hardware

Since 2014, Terrapin Works has provided fabrication, prototyping, and design resources through the University of Maryland's A. James Clark School of Engineering. Its workshops give students room to make, measure, revise, and try again.

The Electric Boat Team brings those tools together around a marine project. Students work across mechanical design, electrical systems, and manufacturing to build and test electric propulsion on the water.

Why electric

Electric motors offer quiet operation without gasoline or engine oil on board. The engineering challenge is to make an entire boat work as a system.

Propulsion, power, structure, controls, and safety all have to come together — and the team builds close to the Chesapeake Bay where it matters.
