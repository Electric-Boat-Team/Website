A Maryland project

Where ideas become hardware

Since 2014, Terrapin Works has provided fabrication, prototyping, and design resources through the University of Maryland's A. James Clark School of Engineering. Its workshops give students room to make, measure, and revise with over 200 machines across 28,000 sq ft of lab space.

The Electric Boat Team applies that toolchain to marine electric propulsion. With the ASNE Promoting Electric Propulsion (PEP) competition as the goal, students design, build, and test a full vessel — gaining close-to-industry engineering experience that is hard to get in a classroom.

Why electric

Electric motors run quietly with no gasoline or engine oil on board. The challenge is making an entire boat work as a system — propulsion, power, structure, controls, and safety all have to come together.

The team builds close to the Chesapeake Bay, where electric propulsion means cleaner waterways and quieter operation.
