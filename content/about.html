A Maryland project

Where ideas become hardware

Since 2014, Terrapin Works has run fabrication, prototyping, and design resources through the University of Maryland's Clark School of Engineering, with over 200 machines across 28,000 sq ft of lab space.

The Electric Boat Team applies that shop to marine electric propulsion, design, build, and test a full vessel for the ASNE PEP competition.

Why electric

Electric motors run quietly with no gasoline or engine oil. The challenge is making the whole boat work as a system, propulsion, power, structure, controls, and safety together.

The team builds near the Chesapeake Bay, where electric propulsion means cleaner waterways and quieter operation.
