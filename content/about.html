About

Terrapin Works is a subunit of Engineering Information Technology in the A. James Clark School of Engineering. Since 2014 it has provided rapid prototyping, advanced manufacturing, and digital design resources to the campus and surrounding community, from 3D printing and CNC machining to composite fabrication and electronics.

The Electric Boat Team applies that toolchain to marine electric propulsion. Students design, fabricate, and test a full vessel, gaining practical engineering, manufacturing, and teamwork experience that is hard to get in a classroom.

Why electric, why now

Small gasoline outboards and inboard engines operate under a far weaker regulatory standard than automotive engines, even though boats often work harder. Electric propulsion removes fuel spills, oil changes over water, fumes, vibration, and noise, and when charged from renewable energy it can be zero-emission from source to wake.

Where we are

The team is based at the University of Maryland in College Park, minutes from the Chesapeake Bay and its tributaries.
